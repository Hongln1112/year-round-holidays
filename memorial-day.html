Memorial Day: Kick Off Summer Right

BBQ menu, parade tips, travel planning, and how to explain the meaning to kids without killing the fun.

Memorial Day weekend is the unofficial start of summer. Three days off, the grill comes out, and everyone goes somewhere. Here's how to enjoy it with kids without the stress.

Part 1

Backyard BBQ Menu

The Classic Spread

  • Main: Burgers, hot dogs, and maybe some chicken. Keep it simple.
  • Sides: Chips and dip, coleslaw, potato salad. Store-bought is fine.
  • Dessert: Watermelon. It's the official Memorial Day dessert.
  • Drinks: Lemonade, iced tea, and plenty of water.
Part 2

What to Tell the Kids

You don't need to give a history lesson.

Age-Appropriate Explanations

  • Toddlers: "This is a special weekend to have fun and be with family."
  • Preschool: "We say thank you to people who helped keep our country safe."
  • Elementary: "Memorial Day is when we remember people who died serving our country. It's also a weekend to spend time with family."
Part 3

Going to a Parade?

  • Arrive 30 minutes early for a good spot.
  • Bring a blanket or chairs. Parades last 1-2 hours.
  • Bring a bag for candy and beads.
  • Sunscreen and water. It's already hot out.
  • Wave at veterans. It means a lot.
Part 4

Traveling With Kids on a Holiday Weekend

Every road trip with kids is a gamble. Here's how to stack the odds.

  • Leave early: Before 6 AM if you can. Kids sleep, traffic hasn't started.
  • Snacks, snacks, snacks: More than you think you need. Granola bars, fruit, crackers.
  • Pit stops: Plan them. Every 2 hours. Let them run around.
  • Download movies: Don't rely on hotel WiFi. Download the night before.
  • Pack a change of clothes: In the car. For spills, for accidents, for everything.
Part 5

Pool and Water Safety

Memorial Day weekend means pools open up. Water safety matters.

  • Never leave kids alone near water. Not even for 10 seconds.
  • Floaties are not life jackets. Get proper coast guard-approved vests.
  • Apply sunscreen before getting in the pool. Reapply every hour.
  • Designate a "water watcher." Rotate adults so nobody zones out.
Part 6

Common Questions

What's the difference between Memorial Day and Veterans Day?▾
Memorial Day honors those who died while serving. Veterans Day honors all veterans, living and dead. Both are important, but they're different.
Should we put flags on the house?▾
It's a nice touch if you have one. Small flags on the porch or in the yard. Not required, but appreciated.
Is it too early for the pool?▾
Depends where you live. In the south, yes. In the north, the water is still freezing. Check the temperature. If it's below 70°F, stick to the sprinkler.
