Mother's Day: Keep It Simple, Make It Meaningful

DIY gifts, at-home brunch, the gift of time, and what moms actually want (versus what we think they want).

The best Mother's Days aren't the fancy ones. They're the ones where mom gets to drink her coffee hot and nobody asks her for anything for two hours. Here's how to make it special without booking a resort.

Part 1

Gifts Kids Can Actually Make

DIY Gift Ideas

  • Handprint apron: Paint kid's hand on a canvas apron. It's the gift moms actually wear.
  • "Why I Love Mom" booklet: Fold paper into a mini book. Each page: one reason (with drawings). It'll make her cry.
  • Plant a seed: Kids decorate a pot, plant a flower. It grows all summer — a reminder all season.
  • Breakfast in bed: Dad leads. Kids carry the tray. Pancakes or pastries, juice, and a flower from the yard.
Part 2

Brunch Without the Wait

At-Home Brunch Menu

  • Main: Overnight French toast casserole. Prep the night before, bake in the morning.
  • Side: Fruit salad. Chop the night before, store in the fridge.
  • Drink: Mimosas for mom, juice for kids.

No reservation, no 2-hour wait, no tipping. Eat in pajamas if you want.

Part 3

What Mom Actually Wants (vs. What We Think She Wants)

Skip the Fancy, Go for the Real

  • She wants: A clean kitchen. Not a vase of flowers on a dirty counter.
  • She wants: To sleep in. Not a 7 AM wake-up call with breakfast in bed.
  • She wants: To not be "on" all day. A few hours alone is worth more than any gift.
  • She wants: Pictures of the kids, not posed ones. Candid shots, blurry ones, whatever.
Part 4

The Gift of Time

The most underrated Mother's Day gift: time off. Give mom a "coupon book": one morning to herself, one dinner out, one nap. No wrapping needed. She'll use every single one.

Part 5

Activities for the Day

If you want to do something together, keep it low-key.

  • Family walk: After brunch, go for a walk at a local park. No agenda, just fresh air.
  • Garden together: If mom likes gardening, let the kids help plant. She'll love it even if it's messy.
  • Movie afternoon: Put on a movie mom wants to watch, make popcorn, and relax.
  • Takeout dinner: Don't make her cook. Don't make her clean. Order her favorite food.
Part 6

Common Questions

What if mom doesn't like being the center of attention?▾
Take the cue. Some moms want a quiet day at home, not a brunch with 20 relatives. Ask her what she wants — and actually listen.
Should we buy something or make something?▾
Both. A small store-bought gift (flowers, coffee) plus a handmade card or craft. The homemade part is what she'll keep. The store-bought part shows you tried.
What if we're celebrating grandma?▾
Bring the kids over, have a simple meal, and let the kids give their handmade cards. Grandma doesn't need a fancy restaurant — she wants the grandkids' company.
