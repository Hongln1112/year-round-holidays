Late August · Early September

Back to School with Kids

Supplies, routines, first-day jitters, and lunch ideas — everything you need to make the transition from summer to school as smooth as possible.

Back to School isn't a holiday, but it's one of the biggest events of the year for families. Late August means supply runs, new routines, first-day outfits, and a mix of excitement and nervousness. Here's how to handle it without losing your mind.

Supplies

School supplies that don't break the bank

Smart shopping tips

  • Wait for the school's supply list — don't buy anything before you know what's actually required.
  • Dollar stores and big-box stores run deep discounts in late July / early August.
  • Reuse last year's backpack, lunchbox, and any unused supplies before buying new.
  • Label everything. Kids lose more items in the first two weeks than the rest of the year combined.
Routine

Reset the morning routine

Start adjusting 1–2 weeks before school

Summer bedtimes slide late. Start shifting wake-up times 15 minutes earlier every few days, so the first day of school isn't a rude awakening.

  • Lay out clothes the night before
  • Pack backpack after dinner, not at 7am
  • Have a simple, protein-heavy breakfast ready (no, cereal doesn't count)
  • Leave 10 extra minutes for hugs and "have a good day" — it sets the tone
First Day

Surviving the first day

For kids

  • Take the classic first-day photo (new outfit, backpack, sign with grade)
  • Talk about feelings: it's normal to be nervous
  • Plan a special after-school snack or activity — something to look forward to

For parents

  • Don't linger at the classroom door — it makes the transition harder
  • Have tissues ready. You'll need them more than they will.
  • Ask open-ended questions after school: "What was the best part?" not "How was school?"

What to skip

  • Don't buy 100 glue sticks. The school already has plenty.
  • Don't schedule big trips in the first week — kids are exhausted.
  • Don't compare your kid's first-day experience to other families'. Every kid adjusts differently.
FAQ

Common questions

When is back to school season? +
Most U.S. schools start between mid-August and after Labor Day (early September). Supply shopping peaks in late July and August.
How do I handle back-to-school anxiety? +
Acknowledge it, don't dismiss it. "I know you're nervous, that's normal." Visit the school before the first day if you can. Keep routines consistent — kids feel safer when they know what's coming.
What makes a good school lunch? +
Protein + something crunchy + something sweet. Think: sandwich, apple slices, cheese stick, a cookie. Keep it simple — fancy lunches get thrown away.
