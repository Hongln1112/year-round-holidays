About Year-Round Holidays

We're a family that loves holidays — but we also know how stressful they can be. Between the costumes, the cooking, the travel, and the bedtime battles, celebrating with kids can feel like another job.

That's why we built this site: to cut through the chaos and give parents practical, no-nonsense guides for every holiday of the year. No Pinterest-perfect perfectionism. No 20-step recipes you'll never make. Just the stuff that actually works.

What We Believe

• The best holiday is the one where everyone eats and nobody cries.
• You don't need to spend a lot to make memories.
• Safety comes before Instagram photos.
• It's okay to skip the big tradition and stay home in pajamas.

We update our guides every year based on new safety research, reader feedback, and what we're actually doing with our own kids. If you have a question or a tip to share, we'd love to hear from you.
