Labor Day: Close Out Summer Right

BBQ menu, travel tips, end-of-summer activities, and saying goodbye to the season without the back-to-school blues.

Labor Day weekend is the last big hurrah of summer. Three days off, the grill comes out one more time, and then school starts. Here's how to enjoy it with kids before the routine kicks in.

Part 1

The Backyard BBQ Menu

End-of-Summer Spread

  • Main: Burgers, hot dogs, and veggie skewers. Same as Memorial Day — that's the point.
  • Sides: Coleslaw, corn on the cob, chips and dip.
  • Dessert: Watermelon again. It's summer's last stand.
  • Drinks: Lemonade, iced tea, and s'mores by the fire pit at night.
Part 2

What Is Labor Day, Anyway?

A quick explanation you can actually use.

Age-Appropriate Versions

  • Toddlers: "One more long weekend to play outside before school starts."
  • Elementary: "Labor Day is a holiday to celebrate working people. It's also the unofficial end of summer."
  • Older kids: "It started in the late 1800s to honor workers' contributions. Now it's mostly a three-day weekend and a cookout."
Part 3

End-of-Summer Bucket List

  • One last trip to the pool: Before it closes for the season.
  • Pick apples or go berry picking: Fall is starting, even if it doesn't feel like it.
  • Watch the sunset: Pick a park, bring snacks, watch it go down. The kids will remember it.
  • Camp in the backyard: One last tent night before it gets too cold.
  • Take a "summer photo": Same spot every year. Compare to last year.
Part 4

Traveling on Labor Day Weekend

It's one of the busiest travel weekends of the year. Plan accordingly.

  • Leave Thursday night or Friday early: Beat the worst of the traffic.
  • Book hotels now: Prices go up as the weekend gets closer.
  • Pack snacks and downloads: You will be in the car longer than you think.
  • Consider staying local: A nearby lake or state park works just as well.
Part 5

Back-to-School Prep

Labor Day weekend is when it hits you: school is starting.

  • School supplies: Do it this weekend. Stores will be picked over next week.
  • Bedtime adjustment: Start shifting bedtime 15 minutes earlier each night. Ease into it.
  • School clothes: Try everything on. Kids grew over the summer.
  • Don't stress the kids out: They still have the weekend to be kids. Save the prep talk for Sunday evening.
Part 6

Common Questions

Can you wear white after Labor Day?▾
The fashion rule is dead. Wear whatever you want. Nobody cares.
Is it too late to go to the beach?▾
Depends where you live. In most of the country, the water is still warm enough for a quick dip. Just don't plan on a full beach day — the sun is weaker now.
What if we're not traveling?▾
Perfect. Stay home, grill, and enjoy not fighting traffic. The best Labor Days are the low-key ones.
