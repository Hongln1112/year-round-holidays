Easter: Egg Hunts, Baskets, and Brunch

Everything you need for a fun family Easter — egg hunt tips, basket ideas, brunch, and visiting the bunny.

Easter is one of those holidays that hits different with little kids. The egg hunt, the candy, the bunny — it's pure magic. Here's how to pull it off without losing your mind (or your living room).

Part 1

Egg Hunt Tips That Actually Work

Backyard Egg Hunt Plan

  • Age-group the hunt: Little kids get one area, big kids another. No more toddlers crying because a 10-year-old grabbed everything.
  • Hide eggs at different heights: Toddler eggs on the grass, preschool eggs on low bushes, big kid eggs under planters.
  • Have a limit: "Find 10 eggs each" prevents one kid from hoarding 50.
  • Plastic eggs with candy: Fill them the night before. Hide them after kids go to bed.
  • Golden egg: One special egg with a bigger prize (a small toy, extra candy). It makes the hunt memorable.
Part 2

Easter Basket Ideas That Aren't Just Candy

Beyond the Chocolate

  • Sidewalk chalk: Spring weather is here, get outside.
  • Bubbles: Cheap, always a hit.
  • Stickers and coloring books: Quiet time for mom after the sugar rush.
  • A small toy: Matchbox car, stuffed animal, or puzzle.
  • One chocolate bunny: You have to have it. Don't skip the tradition.
Part 3

Easter Brunch Without the Fuss

  • Make-ahead quiche: Prep the night before, bake in the morning.
  • Hot cross buns: Store-bought is fine. No one judges.
  • Fruit platter: Strawberries, grapes, melon. Colorful and fresh.
  • Mimosas for the adults: You earned it after the 7 AM egg hunt.
Part 4

Visiting the Easter Bunny

Is your toddler ready for a 6-foot rabbit?

  • Check the age: Most kids are scared the first time. 2-3 years old is hit or miss.
  • Go early: Before the line gets long and before the kid melts down from waiting.
  • Bring a backup: If they cry, take a picture from a distance and move on. No forcing it.
  • Talk about it first: "The bunny is big and fuzzy, but he's nice." Prepare them.
Part 5

Easter Crafts for the Day

  • Dye eggs: The classic. Use a kit, it's worth the $5.
  • Paper bunny ears: Construction paper, glue, elastic. Toddlers love wearing them all day.
  • Handprint chick: Yellow handprint on paper, add an eye and a beak.
  • Plant grass: Grow wheatgrass in a cup. It sprouts in 3 days and looks like Easter grass.
Part 6

Common Questions

What if my kid is scared of the Easter bunny?▾
Totally normal. Skip the photo op and just do the egg hunt. The bunny doesn't have to be part of the celebration for every family.
How many eggs should we hide?▾
About 10-15 per kid. Any more and it's chaos. Any less and it's over in 2 minutes.
Should we do a religious service or just the fun stuff?▾
Whatever fits your family. The egg hunt and brunch are fun regardless of why you celebrate. Do what feels right.
