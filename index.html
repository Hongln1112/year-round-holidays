Common holiday questions

Quick answers to the things people search for every year.

When is Mother's Day in 2026? +
Mother's Day is always the second Sunday of May. In 2026, that's May 10, 2026.
When is Father's Day in 2026? +
Father's Day is always the third Sunday of June. In 2026, that's June 21, 2026.
Is Juneteenth a federal holiday? +
Yes. Juneteenth (June 19) became a federal holiday in 2021. Government offices and many employers close. Private companies vary.
What is the most celebrated holiday in America? +
Christmas is the most widely celebrated holiday in the U.S., followed by Thanksgiving and the Fourth of July.
Is Cinco de Mayo Mexican Independence Day? +
No. Mexico's Independence Day is September 16. Cinco de Mayo commemorates the 1862 Battle of Puebla and is more widely celebrated in the U.S. than in most of Mexico.
When is Easter in 2026? +
In 2026, Easter Sunday falls on April 5. Easter is always on the first Sunday after the first full moon following the spring equinox.
